The Platform

What is Picpool?

Picpool (formerly Picload) is a web application built for image lovers who want a clean, distraction-free way to display and share their work. During active development the platform was hosted at picload.netlify.app, showcasing a responsive gallery, per-photo detail pages, and a focused set of organization tools.

What it does

Features

Seamless sharing

Upload, organize, and share images with a modern, minimal interface.

Responsive gallery

A beautiful, adaptive gallery view that looks great on any screen.

Mobile-friendly

Browsing and management tuned for touch, wherever you are.

Smart organization

Tagging, search, sorting, and categories help keep every photo findable.

Get involved

Contributing & Team Opportunities

Picpool is built on community and collaboration. If you're interested in supporting the platform through photography, curation, or platform stewardship, we'd love to hear from you. Contributions are accepted on a rolling basis and remain open throughout our active development phase.

Photo submissions

We welcome image submissions from the community. Please include a photo title, a brief description, and your preferred credit information. All contributors are credited within the image descriptions on the platform.

Administrator applications

Are you interested in a more involved role within the Picpool ecosystem? We are currently accepting applications for administrator positions. To apply, please submit your application to our official correspondence channel at picload2@gmail.com, outlining your relevant experience, areas of interest, and proposed contributions to the platform.

We review all applications carefully and will respond to qualified candidates as opportunities become available.
